# Prompts para reemplazar las imágenes de Describe the Image

Generá cada imagen con el prompt correspondiente (Gemini/ImageFX, ChatGPT/DALL-E,
Midjourney, etc.) y guardala con el nombre EXACTO indicado, en:

`public/images/describe/`

Sobrescribiendo el archivo existente. No hace falta tocar ningún código —
la app ya referencia esos nombres de archivo.

Sufijo común para todos los prompts (agregalo al final de cada uno):

> photorealistic, professional editorial photography, realistic commercial
> photography, high-resolution, natural lighting, real people, real
> environment, natural expressions, professional composition, shallow
> depth of field. Avoid: cartoon, illustration, 3D render, anime, vector
> art, fantasy, surreal, deformed hands, extra fingers, distorted faces,
> readable text, low resolution, blurry, pixelated.

## 1. office-meeting.png
**Prompt:** A realistic photo of a man and a woman having a conversation
at a desk in a modern office, one gesturing while speaking and the other
listening attentively, an open laptop and some papers on the desk between
them, large windows with natural daylight in the background, a small
potted plant nearby. Mid-shot, both people fully visible.

## 2. coffee-shop.png
**Prompt:** A realistic photo inside a modern coffee shop: a barista behind
the counter preparing a drink with an espresso machine, a customer waiting
at the counter with a bag over their shoulder, a chalkboard menu on the
wall (writing not legible/blurred), a few cups on the counter. Natural
window light, warm tones.

## 3. airport-checkin.png
**Prompt:** A realistic photo of an airport check-in area: an airline
agent behind the counter helping a traveller who has a rolling suitcase,
a departures board visible in the background (screen content blurred/not
legible), other passengers slightly out of focus in the background.
Bright, modern terminal lighting.

## 4. restaurant.png
**Prompt:** A realistic photo of a restaurant dinner scene: two people
sitting at a table with plates, glasses and a lit candle, mid-conversation,
and a waiter approaching with a tray in the background. Warm ambient
lighting, softly blurred restaurant interior behind them.

## 5. job-interview.png
**Prompt:** A realistic photo of a job interview in an office: an
interviewer sitting behind a desk gesturing while talking, a candidate
sitting across from them listening, a laptop and a folder/CV on the desk,
a bookshelf and window in the background. Natural light, professional
office setting.

## Alternativa sin IA: bancos de fotos gratuitos
Si preferís no generar y usar fotos reales con licencia libre, buscá estos
términos en Unsplash o Pexels (ambos permiten uso comercial sin
atribución obligatoria):

- office-meeting.png → "coworkers office conversation desk"
- coffee-shop.png → "barista customer coffee shop counter"
- airport-checkin.png → "airport check-in counter traveler"
- restaurant.png → "couple dinner restaurant table waiter"
- job-interview.png → "job interview office desk"

Descargá en buena resolución (idealmente ≥1600px de ancho), y guardá cada
una con el nombre de archivo exacto de la lista de arriba.
